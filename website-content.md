## 6. Patient Education Initiative: `@eyecarewith_rs`

Dr. Ruchita Sontakke created the digital eye wellness initiative **@eyecarewith_rs** on Instagram to empower patients with authentic, actionable eye care guidance.

### Featured Educational Topics
1. **The 20-20-20 Rule for Digital Eye Strain**:
   - *Rule*: Every 20 minutes of screen use, pause and look at an object 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
   - *Clinical Mechanism*: Relaxes the ciliary accommodation muscles, stimulates involuntary complete blinks, and re-lubricates the corneal tear film.
2. **Myth vs. Fact: Can Carrots Cure Refractive Errors?**:
   - *Fact*: Vitamin A / beta-carotene is essential for rhodopsin in retinal photoreceptors, but it cannot change the anatomical axial length or corneal curvature of the eyeball.
3. **Contact Lens Hygiene & Acanthamoeba Keratitis Prevention**:
   - *Golden Rule*: Never expose contact lenses or lens storage cases to tap water, shower water, or pool water. Sterile solutions prevent sight-threatening amoebic and fungal keratitis.
4. **Cataract Early Warning Signs**:
   - *Symptoms*: Frequent change in reading glasses, starbursts or halos around oncoming headlights at night, loss of color vibrancy, and cloudiness.
